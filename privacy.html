我们不收集任何数据

  • 本应用不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享您的任何个人信息。
  • 本应用没有账号系统，无需注册或登录。

游戏进度、设置（如声音开关）与战绩记录仅保存在您设备的本地存储中，不会离开您的设备。删除本应用会一并删除这些数据。

儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何用户数据，因此也不会收集儿童的个人信息。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

XiYou JiangYaoJi (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

No Data Collection

  • The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.
  • There is no account system; no registration or login is required.
  • The App runs fully offline and never connects to any server.
  • The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Data Stored on Your Device

Game progress, settings (such as the sound toggle), and battle records are stored only in local storage on your device and never leave it. Deleting the App removes this data.

Children’s Privacy

The App collects no user data of any kind, and therefore collects no personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
