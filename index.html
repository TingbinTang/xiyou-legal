常见问题与联系方式

游戏没有声音？

请先检查设备音量与侧边静音开关，并确认游戏内「设置」中的声音开关已打开。

游戏需要联网吗？

不需要。本游戏可以完全离线游玩。

我的进度保存在哪里？卸载会丢失吗？

进度、设置与战绩保存在您的设备本地；删除游戏会一并清除这些数据，无法恢复。

遇到闪退或卡顿怎么办？

可以尝试关闭后台其他应用并重启设备。若问题仍然存在，请邮件联系我们，并附上设备型号与系统版本，我们会尽快处理。

Support

Frequently asked questions and contact

No sound in the game?

Check your device volume and the side mute switch, then make sure the sound toggle in the in-game Settings is on.

Does the game require an internet connection?

No. The game is fully playable offline.

Where is my progress stored? Will I lose it if I delete the app?

Progress, settings, and battle records are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this data permanently.

The game crashes or lags — what should I do?

Try closing other apps and restarting your device. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and OS version.
